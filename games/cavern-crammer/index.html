CAVERN CRAMMER

a tiny pixel roguelike of ruins, riddles & recall

Climb tall, crumbling ruins full of spikes, foes, wall-jump shafts, and hidden traps. Coins you carry are only safe once you reach a shrine — die before then and they're gone. Each ruin hides one locked vault door leading to a loot room; find its key at a shrine to claim the treasure. Clear 4 ruins in a zone and a Guardian blocks the way — defeat it to descend into a new, deadlier zone. You have limited lives — run out, and the ruins are lost for good (though your shop upgrades remain).

◀ ▶ or A/D — walk  •  SPACE / ▲ / W — jump (tap again mid-air to double jump, hold toward a wall to wall-jump)
