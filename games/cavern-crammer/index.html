DESCRIPTION

🏮 Climb tall, crumbling ruins lit only by your lantern.

🪙 Coins you carry are only safe once banked at a shrine — die before then and they're gone.

🗝️ Each ruin hides a locked vault door; find its key at a shrine to claim the loot.

📚 Answer shrine riddles correctly to earn powerful boons for the rest of your run.

👹 Clear 4 ruins in a zone and a Guardian blocks the way to the next, deadlier zone.
