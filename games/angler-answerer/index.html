HOW TO PLAY

  1. Answer questions to earn bait — every correct answer gives 1 bait and no coins; an incorrect answer costs 5 shared coins.
  2. Cast your line: hold to charge your power, release to throw.
  3. Hook it: when a fish bites, tap fast before it gets away.
  4. Reel it in: hold to raise the marker and keep it inside the moving catch zone — that's what pulls the distance bar down to zero.
  5. Level up to unlock new fishing grounds, discover every species for your Fishdex, and sell harder catches for modestly higher coin rewards in the Cooler.
